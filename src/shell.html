    Getting started

    • Choose a scenario from the menu at the top, or edit the values under Setup and press Apply.
    • The side panel describes the scenario, lists the orbital elements and holds the display options.
    • The lower panel has diagnostics, graphs, guided experiments, sweeps, comparisons and a report.

    Keyboard

    • Space play or pause
    • In the orbit view: arrow keys pan, + − zoom, 0 fit
    • Esc closes menus and the tour

    Model

    • Two point masses, Newtonian gravity, Velocity Verlet in SI units. Motion is computed in the orbital plane (2D).
    • Dashed curves are exact Kepler conics from the initial conditions; solid paths are the integration.
    • Inclination and node orient that plane in 3D for the orbit-plane view and ground track; they do not change two-body motion.
    • The J2 option adds analytic secular drift of the node and perigee for Earth orbits. It is separate from the integrator.
    • Transfer burns are instantaneous velocity changes applied between integration steps.
    • Body textures, comet tails and the star background are visual only.
